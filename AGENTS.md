# Product and design direction

LofAI is a chill, vibey productivity space people should enjoy returning to.
The UX should feel fun, quirky, full of character, and alive. Carry this
direction through every feature, including settings, integrations, empty
states, loading, and errors.

- Build on the existing cat, dot-matrix artwork, theme palettes, and tactile
  controls. Reuse shared components; duration sliders use `FineDial`, as the
  music player does.
- Give actions small, meaningful reactions: an ear perk, a soft landing, a
  satisfying check. Keep focus time calm and respect reduced motion and the
  app's low-power rendering budget.
- Use warm, concise copy while keeping action names and service names clear.
- Make returning welcoming. Celebrate effort and rest; avoid guilt, punitive
  streaks, or a companion that suffers when someone takes time away.
- Keep the workspace easy to settle into. Reveal secondary tools in context
  and make integrations bring a manageable selection of work into the room.
- Distinguish local changes, imports, and synchronized changes clearly. Keep
  local tasks usable when a connected service is unavailable.

See [the productivity direction](docs/PRODUCTIVITY.md) for the current tool
inventory, proposed additions, and integration priorities. Proposals there
are planning guidance, not a claim that those features already exist.
